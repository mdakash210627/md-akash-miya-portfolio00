Biography

I am a B.Sc. Engineering graduate in Information and Communication Engineering from Pabna University of Science and Technology, with a CGPA of 3.47 out of 4.00. My research interests span artificial intelligence, deep learning, computer vision, biomedical imaging, signal processing, and explainable AI.

My undergraduate thesis focuses on annotation-guided, uncertainty-aware explainable multi-task learning for active pulmonary tuberculosis detection and lesion localisation. My research work also covers PPG-based health monitoring, machine learning for disease detection, and deepfake detection.

Alongside research, I work as an ICT Instructor at Srizon Coaching Center, Pabna, where I teach ICT, prepare learning materials, and support students in developing problem-solving skills.
